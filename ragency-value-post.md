```
Agencies losing leads to slow follow-up: the 3 cheapest fixes before you buy any tool

Ran this as a self-audit on a test pipeline (fake data, but the failure modes are the ones I keep seeing described here):

1. No dedupe rule. Same lead enters 2–3× via form + ad + email, reps chase ghosts, reporting lies. Fix: search-before-create on email at every entry point. One rule, kills a whole class of "CRM is a mess" complaints.

2. No after-hours branch. ~40% of inbound arrives outside 9–5 and waits until morning. Fix: night/weekend path → instant SMS + 2-hour SLA task. This is usually the highest-ROI 30 minutes of automation work in the building.

3. No failure alerts on the automations themselves. The zap/scenario fails silently for 3 weeks and nobody notices until a client asks where their leads went. Fix: error notification on every workflow + a weekly 5-minute log skim. Unsexy, worth more than any AI scoring layer.

What I deliberately did NOT do: lead scoring, AI replies, dashboards. Scoring a leaking funnel is polishing a bucket with holes — fix 1–3 first, then score.

If useful I can post the 12-point version of this checklist I use (covers assignment, SLAs, consent basis). Say the word.
```
